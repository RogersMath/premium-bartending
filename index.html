Questions hosts ask.

How far ahead should I book?

As early as you can. Palm Beach's busiest season runs roughly November through April, and weekend dates go first. If you have a date, send it now and Daina will confirm availability.

Who buys the alcohol?

Most hosts buy their own bottles. Daina gives you a shopping list sized to your guest count and menu, so you buy only what you will pour. If you would like a different arrangement, ask when you request your quote.

How many bartenders do I need?

A good rule is one bartender for every 50 guests. Daina will recommend a number based on your menu, your venue and how quickly you want the bar to move.

Can you make drinks for guests who don't drink?

Yes. Every menu includes zero-proof drinks made with the same care as the cocktails. That matters for graduation parties with guests under 21, and for anyone skipping alcohol.

Where do you work?

Events across Palm Beach County, including Palm Beach, West Palm Beach, Jupiter and Boca Raton. For an event farther out, ask and Daina will let you know.
